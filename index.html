About the College

Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya Adarsha Mahavidyalaya (PDUAM), Amjonga is a Government model college established in 2017. The college offers Higher Secondary (HS) courses under ASSEB Div II and the Four Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) in line with NEP 2020 under Gauhati University. The College has Arts and Science Streams

Purpose of this Site

This site gives students and faculty members quick, mobile-friendly access to the class routine, GU holiday list, notices, curriculum resources, and examination documents — all in one place.
